### Signal 1 — Agentic AI is producing measurable CX outcomes in live telecom deployments (not just pilots)

**The finding:**
A TM Forum Catalyst Project consortium (Vodafone, TELUS, Verizon, Telecom Argentina, Converge, and six other partners — 11 organisations in total) demonstrated the following outcomes from an agentic AI architecture applied to proactive customer care:

- **50–70% reduction** in incoming call-centre inquiries related to billing
- **15–25% reduction** in churn rates
- **15% reduction** in operating expenses
- **5% revenue uplift** from enhanced upsell and cross-sell activity

**Source:** [How agentic AI can transform customer care in telecom and beyond — TM Forum Inform](https://inform.tmforum.org/features-and-opinion/how-agentic-ai-can-transform-customer-care-in-telecom-and-beyond)
**Publication date:** 6 November 2025
**Verification status:** ✅ **Verified at primary source** (TM Forum Inform, fetched in this session)
**Sample / methodology note:** Outcomes from a single consortium proof-of-concept catalyst project, not a broad industry survey — Strategist should frame as "demonstrated in live operator deployments" rather than an industry-wide average.

**Northgate fit:** Northgate's Digital Transformation and Process & Solution Consulting practices sit exactly at the intersection of BSS/OSS integration and CX architecture — this signal directly names the operational layer (billing inquiries, churn, OpEx) that operators ask Northgate to help with.

### Signal 2 — The measurement gap: most CX leaders cannot convert satisfaction data into financial impact

**The finding:**
- **96%** of companies measure customer satisfaction
- Only **20%** translate that satisfaction data into quantifiable financial impact or ROI

Additionally:
- **92%** of surveyed companies consider CX a high priority
- **42%** cite rigid internal processes as the single biggest obstacle to CX improvement (outranking technology or budget)
- **54%** have a dedicated CX team
- **83%** of CX experts expect CX investment to grow

**Source:** [Deloitte Digital CX Study 2025](https://www.deloittedigital.com/de/en/insights/research/deloitte-cx-study-2025.html)
**Publication date:** 24 October 2025
**Sample:** 150 CX professionals, supplemented by qualitative expert interviews. **Caveat: German companies only** — Strategist should consider whether to localise or present as an illustrative benchmark rather than a global figure.
**Verification status:** ✅ **Verified at primary source** (Deloitte Digital Germany, fetched in this session)

**Northgate fit:** The 20%/96% split is a classic process-not-technology failure — operators have the data but lack the closed loop from insight to action. This sits inside Northgate's Process & Solution Consulting and Commercial Excellence practices.

### Signal 3 — Rigid internal processes, not technology, are the primary CX barrier

**The finding:** 42% of CX professionals cite rigid internal processes as the biggest obstacle to improving CX — ranking above insufficient investment willingness (37%).

**Source:** Deloitte Digital CX Study 2025 (same as Signal 2 above)
**Verification status:** ✅ **Verified at primary source**

**Note for Strategist:** This is a sub-finding within Signal 2 but strong enough to stand alone as its own signal in the "7 signals" format. In a telecom context, "rigid internal processes" maps directly to the eTOM process fragmentation and legacy BSS/OSS layers Northgate knows well — this isn't generic corporate-speak; it has a specific operational identity in telco.

### Signal 4 — The loyalty vacuum: consumers feel no binding loyalty to their telecom provider

**The finding:**
- **77%** of consumers report feeling no loyalty to their telecom provider
- **47%** remain with their primary operator for more than five years
- **22% annual churn rate** across telecom providers
- Loyalty programmes can increase customer sentiment by up to **15%** and reduce churn by approximately **8%**

**Source:** [2026 Telecommunications Industry Outlook — Deloitte Insights](https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/insights/industry/technology/technology-media-telecom-outlooks/telecommunications-industry-outlook.html)
**Verification status:** ⚠️ **Partially verified** — figures appeared on the Deloitte page (fetched in this session), but the fetch could not confirm sample sizes, underlying survey methodology, or original publication dates for each specific figure. Deloitte's page references "industry research and customer gauge benchmarks" without naming the primary source of each number.
**Strategist instruction:** These are usable as directional context but should NOT be presented with precise attribution in post copy. Use as framing for the "loyalty vacuum" concept; do not lead the post's argument on these figures.

**Northgate fit:** Loyalty and churn are the commercial consequence of CX failure in telecom — directly in scope for Northgate's Commercial Excellence practice and any NPS programme work.

### Signal 5 — CX investment is broadly expected to grow, but organisational structure lags intent

**The finding:**
- **83%** of CX experts expect CX investment to grow (35% expect strong growth, 48% expect slight growth)
- Yet only **54%** of companies have a dedicated CX team responsible for CX

**Source:** Deloitte Digital CX Study 2025 (same primary source as Signals 2 and 3)
**Verification status:** ✅ **Verified at primary source**

**Strategist note:** The tension between "83% expect more investment" and "only 54% have a team to own it" is a useful structural irony for a LinkedIn post. Pairs well as a counterpoint to the loyalty vacuum (Signal 4) — operators know they must invest but haven't yet built the internal muscle to convert spend into experience.

### Signal 6 — From reactive to proactive care: AI agents are redefining the architecture of customer support

**The finding (conceptual, supported by TM Forum verified data above):**
The TM Forum Catalyst project (Signal 1) demonstrates a three-layer agentic architecture — concierge agent (monitors subscriber activity), orchestration agent (routes inquiries), and domain-specific agents (functional resolution). This marks a structural shift from CRM-driven reactive queuing to always-on, intent-aware care.

**Supporting context (unverified, from search results only):**
- Telefónica's Aura reportedly handles over 400 million interactions annually across 30+ channels, now augmented with generative AI — **unverified**, source: search result summary only. Do not cite with attribution.

**Verification status for the architectural concept:** The three-agent framework is ✅ **verified via TM Forum primary source**. The Telefónica statistic is ❌ **unverified** — exclude from post copy.

**Northgate fit:** This directly maps to Northgate's BSS/OSS vocabulary — agentic AI in this context operates on billing systems, provisioning events, and network fault escalations, not just chatbot scripts. Northgate can speak to this with operational credibility that a generalist firm cannot.

### Signal 7 — Network experience is the product: CX and network performance are now inseparable

**The finding (search-result leads, primary source not fetched):**
McKinsey research reportedly found that 38% of telecom churn is linked directly to network performance issues, and AI-driven CX intelligence can identify customers who are up to five times more likely to churn in response to poor network experience.

**Source lead:** [An AI-driven approach to measuring and maximising telco customer experience — McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/technology-media-and-telecommunications/our-insights/the-network-is-the-product-how-ai-can-put-telco-customer-experience-in-focus)
**Verification status:** ❌ **Unverified** — McKinsey page timed out on fetch; no retry attempted (budget cap reached). Figures came from secondary search-result summaries. Do not cite these numbers in post copy with attribution. The article title and URL are confirmed as real McKinsey content.

**Strategist note:** The conceptual angle — that network experience *is* the CX, not a back-end input to it — is strongly supported by the verified TM Forum material (Signal 1 shows churn reduction tied to proactive network-aware care). Strategist can use this conceptual signal without relying on the unverified McKinsey numbers. The URL is worth a manual fetch by the human team before the post is finalised.

## Recommended construction logic for Strategist

The "7 signals" post format maps well to the verified data here if constructed as follows:

- **Signals 1, 2, 3** carry the post's factual weight — all three have primary-source verified numbers.
- **Signals 4 and 5** provide commercial/organisational framing — Signal 4 is directionally solid but needs soft attribution language; Signal 5 pairs with 2 and 3 as a system.
- **Signal 6** gives the post an architectural forward-look that Northgate can own with operational vocabulary (BSS/OSS, billing events, provisioning) — no statistics needed; the TM Forum framework is the evidence.
- **Signal 7** works as a conceptual anchor (the network is the product) without requiring the unverified McKinsey stats — it ties Signals 1 and 6 together.

This gives exactly seven signals, four with hard verified numbers, two with conceptual/framework backing, one with directional context only.
