## 1. How I read the opportunity

- **It's an A2P monetization and trust story, not a fraud story.** Orange Romania's
  decision-makers buy this if it protects and grows enterprise-messaging margin. Fraud
  reduction is the political cover and the regulatory hook — not the ROI line.
- **The hard part is ecosystem orchestration, not technology.** TCR only creates value once
  brands, CSPs, CNPs and DCAs actually register — a cold-start problem that needs operator
  leverage and, ideally, an ANCOM / DNSC tailwind. Aligning that many parties on one standard
  is a telecom BD and transformation problem.
- **Romania is the proof point, not the prize.** A clean Orange Romania pilot is the reference
  that unlocks Orange Group (~20 markets) and becomes the template for Vodafone, Deutsche
  Telekom and Telefónica.
