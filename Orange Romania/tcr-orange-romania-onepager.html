Case study — Tier-1 European mobile operator

A sender registry that turned grey A2P into billable traffic

The operator was terminating a large share of enterprise SMS via grey routes and unregistered senders — no visibility of who was sending, revenue leaking.

The challenge

No line of sight on A2P

  • Grey-route & unregistered enterprise traffic
  • Sender identity unknown per message
  • Leaking A2P revenue, rising smishing

What we built

Registry + firewall policy

  • Central sender & campaign registry, identity verified by role
  • Integrated to the messaging firewall to act on registration status
  • Feedback loop of violation metrics by sender

My role — Solution & Scope Lead

Workshop to go-live

  • Ran discovery workshops to a signed end-to-end solution
  • Owned pilot scope: features, acceptance criteria, business KPIs
  • Bridged operator commercial teams and the vendor’s technical team; drove UAT & go-live

Outcome

Grey-route A2P brought under registration and billing

Traffic converted from grey to registered, billable routes — and a repeatable model for the next markets.
